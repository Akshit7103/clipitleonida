ClipItLeonida Clipper

A personal, single-user tool that turns short gaming live-stream highlights into vertical videos and publishes them to one YouTube channel, Clip It Leonida.

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy and our Privacy Policy.

What it does

  1. Watches a short list of public live streams, prioritising creators whose clipping programmes invite clippers to post clips of their streams.
  2. Finds highlight moments from live chat activity and cuts a short clip with captions in a vertical layout.
  3. A review step checks every clip (accuracy, safety and the creator programme's rules) before anything is published.
  4. Approved clips are uploaded to the channel owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API (videos.insert), after the owner signs in once with Google and grants the youtube.upload permission.

Who uses it

Only the owner of the Clip It Leonida channel. It is not offered to the public, does not create accounts for other people and does not access anyone else's YouTube account or data.
