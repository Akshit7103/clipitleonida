Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

ClipItLeonida Clipper uses YouTube API Services. Your use of the app is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy (http://www.google.com/policies/privacy) and the YouTube Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

ClipItLeonida Clipper ("the app") is a personal tool run by Akshit Mahajan for one YouTube channel, Clip It Leonida. It is used only by the channel owner. Contact: akshit.mahajan713@gmail.com.

2. Information accessed through YouTube API Services

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests only these permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's own channel.
  • youtube.readonly: to read the owner's own channel name and the status of uploaded videos.

The app does not access, collect or store any other user's YouTube account, channel, videos, comments, subscribers or personal information.

3. How information is used, stored and shared

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file, and is used only to upload the owner's videos and read their status.
  • For each upload the app keeps, on the owner's computer, the video title, description, YouTube video ID and upload time, so the owner can see what was posted.
  • No information obtained through YouTube API Services is sold, shared with or disclosed to third parties, and none is used for advertising.
  • The app places no cookies and does not track visitors to this website.

4. Deletion of data

  • Upload records kept by the app are deleted automatically within 7 days, and any data obtained through YouTube API Services is never kept for more than 30 days.
  • You can ask for any stored data to be deleted at any time by emailing akshit.mahajan713@gmail.com. It will be deleted within 7 days.

5. Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time through the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the app from uploading or reading any data.

6. Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
